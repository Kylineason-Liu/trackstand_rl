INERTIAL-SENSOR ERROR MODEL (standard taxonomy; reference for the IMU DR rework)

  1) DETERMINISTIC (calibratable):
  | #  | component                        | what it is                                              | removable?                            |
  |----|----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
  | 1  | turn-on / initial bias           | constant offset per power cycle (gyro: deg/s; accel: mg)| yes — stationary boot cal (residual ~ noise/√N) |
  | 2  | scale-factor error               | sensitivity error, % of reading (typ. ±0.5%)            | yes — factory/boot cal                |
  | 3  | nonlinearity                     | residual after scale cal                                | mostly — high-order cal               |
  | 4  | misalignment / cross-axis        | axes not orthogonal / not aligned to reference frame    | yes — factory or static-gravity cal   |
  | 5  | g-sensitivity (gyro)             | gyro output shifts under linear acceleration            | partly — needs g-matrix cal           |
  | 6  | vibration rectification (accel)  | vibration induces bias-like accel shift while riding    | NO — depends on ride vibration        |
  | 7  | quantization                     | ADC/FSR resolution floor                                | bounded by FSR choice                 |

  2) STOCHASTIC (unremovable):
  | #  | component                            | what it is                                          | Allan curve signature          |
  |----|--------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
  | 8  | white noise (ARW gyro / VRW accel)   | per-sample fluctuation; datasheet: 0.07 deg/s RMS gyro / 0.001 g RMS accel | slope −½ at small τ |
  | 9  | bias instability                     | slow 1/f random WANDER of the bias                  | FLAT MINIMUM of Allan deviation|
  | 10 | rate / accel random walk             | long-term integrated drift                           | rising at large τ              |

  3) ENVIRONMENTAL:
  | #  | component           | what it is                                                   | removable?                       |
  |----|---------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
  | 11 | temperature effects | bias & scale-factor drift with temp + power-on transient     | partly — temp comp + warm-up    |

HOW TO DETERMINE gyro_res_bias_std AND accel_res_bias_std (both = the std, ACROSS POWER CYCLES,
  of the bias RESIDUAL left after the firmware's calibration — the measurement must include the
  real calibration, or you measure a value you will never experience):
  gyro_res_bias_std (rad/s):
    1. Replicate the firmware's calibration: stationary on the stand, average N samples over the
       same window the firmware would use (e.g. 2-5 s at the control rate) -> that mean is the
       boot's bias estimate, which calibration subtracts. If the firmware already calibrates,
       log its post-calibration output instead.
    2. Residual per boot = 0 - estimate (stationary -> true rate 0). Only the SPREAD across boots
       matters, not any single value.
    3. Repeat over 10-30 COLD power cycles, spread over hours/days to sample thermal variation.
    4. gyro_res_bias_std = std of the per-boot residuals, per axis (use the worst axis).
    5. SANITY: a working calibration lands near max(noise/sqrt(N), thermal) ~ 1e-4..1e-3 rad/s.
       If it comes out ~0.01-0.1 rad/s, the calibration is not doing its job — and that number
       is exactly the margin you lack (this is the killer term; the measurement earns its time).
  accel_res_bias_std (gravity units, dimensionless; 0.02 ~ 1.15 deg):
    1. Stationary, LEVELED on the stand, post warm-up, after the firmware's accel calibration.
    2. Record the accelerometer's gravity vector (g units); residual = measured - true, with
       true = the bike-frame vertical from the leveled stand.
    3. Per boot, take the gravity-component residual; across boots, accel_res_bias_std = its std
       (both channels share one std in the sim — use the conservative/worst component).
    4. Mount misalignment is a PER-UNIT constant: a gravity-referenced boot calibration absorbs
       it automatically (that is the point); if the fleet has several bikes, include several
       boards to sample unit-to-unit spread.
  SHORTCUTS until measured: (a) firmware does NOT calibrate -> use the manual's uncalibrated
    initial-bias spec (may exceed 0.03); (b) firmware DOES calibrate -> noise_RMS/sqrt(N)
    (~0.0012/sqrt(N) rad/s) as a conservative placeholder.
  ONE BENCH SESSION COVERS EVERYTHING: the long stationary log for the Allan-variance test
    yields the per-boot means (gyro residual), the gravity residuals (accel), AND the per-sample
    std (#8 verification) — three numbers, one afternoon. After filling the values in, re-run
    the gyro-bias magnitude sweep around the measured value to confirm the policy's tolerance
    cliff sits comfortably above the residual.

8. physx.usda sets maxJointVelocity 100 rad/s (5729.578 deg/s) on the

9. GPU patch-based friction (friction_offset_threshold 0.04,

## Collision meshes in NewBike-Balance-v1 (2026-09-13)

- The runtime wheel colliders = ONE CONVEX HULL per collision mesh: the URDF
  converter authored `token physics:approximation = "convexHull"` on every
  link's collision prim in payloads/instances.usda (with PhysicsCollisionAPI
  + MeshCollisionAPI). NEW_BIKE_BASE_CFG sets no mesh-collision override, so
  the USD's token stands — this is the provenance of the hull/vertex-walk
  contact the sim-to-sim campaign has been matching against.
- The cfg CAN override the approximation via mesh_approximation_name
  (boundingCube | boundingSphere | convexDecomposition | convexHull | none |
  meshSimplification | sdf — verified in isaaclab/sim/schemas/schemas.py).
  PhysX has NO ellipsoid/cylinder primitive collider.
- The MuJoCo deploy's collisions are hand-authored primitives (model.xml:
  frame box, fork box, wheel ellipsoids); its meshes are visual-only.
- Representation gap: the two engines' common wheel shapes do not overlap.
  Fix paths: boundingSphere on both (cheap, retrain), SDF on both (true
  geometry, plumbing, retrain), or keep fitting MuJoCo (status quo).
