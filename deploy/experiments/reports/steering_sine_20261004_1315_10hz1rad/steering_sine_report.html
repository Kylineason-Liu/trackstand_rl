Methods and limitations · Tracking statistics · Three-cycle tracking overlay

Alignment: C2 uses elapsed_s; position uses position_elapsed_s; velocity uses velocity_elapsed_s; torque uses current_elapsed_s. These are recorder host-receipt times, not hardware acquisition or execution times. No phase shifts, smoothing, or extrapolated targets are applied. C2 targets are drawn as steps between observed frames.

Position conversion: command radians = (C2 target count − 1410) × -0.000383495196971. This mapping is verified against every finite logged unwrapped-position sample. Both position panels have identical vertical scales. Positive means right in the logged coordinates.

Torque: logged signed A1 Q-current × B0 torque constant; not an independent shaft-torque measurement. Grey shading denotes times outside the observed C2-command interval; it does not imply the motor was disabled.

Feedback gaps: lines are broken and red shading added across receipt gaps longer than 50 ms. No missing measurement is replaced by a guessed value. Recorded C2 targets remain held steps; gaps do not reveal physical behavior inside the interval.
